# Worst Superpower

The most useless superpower imaginable, in one line, for make.fun's `u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe` bounty.

The line: *"I can summon my keys from anywhere — as long as I'm already holding them."* (72 characters)

Summoning is a classic superhero power — reach out across any distance and the thing you want flies into your hand. This one keeps the grand setup ("from anywhere") and then cancels it with a single condition: it only works when you are already holding the keys. The power does literally nothing that your hand is not already doing. Picture it in use: a hero striking a dramatic summoning pose, arm outstretched, keys already in his fist. Nothing arrives, nothing changes, and the pose was the whole performance.

It is useless, not a curse — nothing bad happens — and not secretly helpful — holding your keys is not a problem that needs solving. The joke is the condition itself: the one circumstance where the power works is the one circumstance where no power is needed. No slow flight, no invisibility-while-nobody-watches, no familiar gag — a fresh paradox built on one concrete detail (keys, the thing everyone loses and summons in their head already).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
